Stage-A (single-mode):
  ln(area_value)  = b0 + Σ bi * φi(x(m))
  ln(total_value) = c0 + Σ ci * φi(x(m))

Stage-B (reuse ratio):
  ln(rhoA) = d0 + Σ di * ψi(u(M,s))
  ln(rhoP) = e0 + Σ ei * ψi(u(M,s))

Final:
  A_ded_hat = Σ_m exp(f_A(x(m)))
  P_ded_hat = Σ_m exp(f_P(x(m)))
  A_reuse_hat = exp(g_A(u)) * A_ded_hat
  P_reuse_hat = exp(g_P(u)) * P_ded_hat
