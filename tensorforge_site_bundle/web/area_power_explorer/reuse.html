What is measured, and what changes when I click?

18 templates, 630 derived points. The strategies use the paper’s original datapath composition, evaluated at five base K values and seven clock targets. Their area is the sum of constituent unit areas in the released dp_training.csv; this is not a separately synthesized multi-unit wrapper.

Whole-datapath pruning. Deselecting formats removes only datapaths that support none of your required formats. A shared unit keeps its full measured area and its extra format support. Overlapping format support can retain multiple units; this is a projection of each template, not a new optimal circuit. With all formats enabled, the full original composition is retained.

A consistent reference. Savings and the budget use S14, the dedicated arithmetic-FP4 strategy, under the same selected formats, K and clock. LUT-FP4 strategies are explicit alternatives. Negative savings mean the selected template is larger than this reference.

Scope of the comparison. TSMC 22 nm, DC synthesis, released unit measurements. These are the 18 paper templates applied to the bundled measurements; percentages may differ from later clock-gated paper figures. The bundled CSV does not record a gating setting. Clock is a synthesis target; this CSV does not establish timing closure. Area excludes additional integration/interconnect overhead. K follows the paper’s per-format lane scaling, and this page makes no system-throughput or runtime-power claim.

Traceable measurements. Expand any datapath card for its exact CSV line, variant, normalization, accumulator, output and intermediate width. S07, S12 and S16 retain the actual extra BF16 unit even though an older legend omitted BF16 from their shared group.
